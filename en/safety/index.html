Health, safety & environment (HSE)

Safety is the first condition of every lift. Our motto is Safety – Efficiency – Quality – Trust, and safety comes first.

Safety

How we control safety

  1. Lift planningHeavy or complex lifts get a lift plan: crane chosen from its load chart, set-up position, rigging and sequence.
  2. Inspected equipmentCranes and lifting gear are inspected as required by regulation; certificates travel with the equipment.
  3. PeopleOperators and riggers are trained and hold the relevant certificates; site managers supervise on site.
  4. Toolbox talksTasks, risks and signals are briefed before each shift and the lift zone is barricaded.
  5. Workshop maintenanceOur mechanics and electricians service the fleet at the Dinh Vu workshop in Hai Phong.
  6. Ground and weatherGround is assessed and matted with steel plates where needed; lifts stop when wind exceeds the manufacturer's limit.
