Mobile crane rental

Mobile cranes (truck, rough-terrain and all-terrain cranes) travel quickly by road and rig up fast, which suits short lifts and jobs that move between several locations.

Our mobile fleet covers small to large cranes by Kato, Tadano, Liebherr, Demag and Kobelco, serviced at our Dinh Vu workshop in Hai Phong. Larger units are available through partners.

Typical applications

  • Machinery and plant equipment lifting
  • Factory and steel-structure erection
  • Assist cranes for crawler and wind-turbine erection
  • Cargo handling at ports and yards

Manufacturers

How renting works

  1. Send your requestLoad weight and size, lift height, radius, location and dates.
  2. Survey & lift planOur engineers visit the site, choose the crane and plan the lift.
  3. Quotation & contractA clear price per shift, per day or per work package.
  4. MobilisationTransport, crane rig-up and safety checks before lifting.
  5. Execution & hand-overOperators and riggers do the work and hand over on time.
