Truck-mounted cargo crane rental

A cargo crane is a truck with a mounted knuckle or telescopic boom, so one vehicle carries and lifts the load with no extra crane. It is an economical option for medium-weight machinery, structures and materials.

Our cargo-crane trucks carry Soosan, Kanglim, CSS and Dong Yang cranes and serve Hai Phong, industrial zones and inter-provincial routes.

Typical applications

  • Loading and moving machinery and small containers
  • Lifting materials and steelwork on site
  • Factory machine relocation

Manufacturers

  • Soosan
  • Kanglim
  • CSS
  • Dong Yang
  • Dong Feng
