How renting works

  1. Send your requestLoad weight and size, lift height, radius, location and dates.
  2. Survey & lift planOur engineers visit the site, choose the crane and plan the lift.
  3. Quotation & contractA clear price per shift, per day or per work package.
  4. MobilisationTransport, crane rig-up and safety checks before lifting.
  5. Execution & hand-overOperators and riggers do the work and hand over on time.
