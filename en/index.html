Frequently asked questions

Where does Binh Tien rent out cranes?

Our office and workshop are in Hai Phong. We have worked in most provinces from Quang Ninh to Ho Chi Minh City and in Laos, and can mobilise equipment nationwide.

Do you handle heavy and oversized lifts?

Yes. Besides our own crawler and mobile cranes, we work with partners to supply large cranes and prepare a lift plan for each job.

Do rentals include an operator?

Yes. Cranes are rented with Binh Tien operators and riggers for safety and productivity.

What information do you need for a quick quotation?

Load weight and dimensions, lift height, radius (distance from crane position to set-down point), location, schedule and site photos if available.

Need a crane for your next job?

Send us the load, lift height and location. Our engineers will propose the right crane and quote within one working day.
