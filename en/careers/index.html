Careers: crane operators, riggers, mechanics

Our operators, riggers and technicians are the core of Binh Tien. We welcome applications for the roles below at any time.

Careers

Open roles

  • Crawler and mobile crane operator

    Relevant crane operator certificate; willing to travel to sites nationwide.

  • Rigger / signaller

    Slinging, signalling and working with operators to the lift plan.

  • Mechanical, hydraulic and electrical technician

    Servicing cranes and forklifts at Dinh Vu workshop and on site.

  • Tractor and low-bed driver

    Appropriate driving licence; inter-provincial heavy equipment transport.

  • Lifting engineer / site manager

    Lift planning, site supervision, working with local and international contractors.
